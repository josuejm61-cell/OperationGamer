# Bungie

## Overview

Bungie is an American video game developer founded in 1991. The studio became famous for creating the Halo series before developing Destiny and Destiny 2. Bungie is known for its engaging first-person shooters, memorable worlds, and strong online multiplayer experiences.

---

## Contributions to Gaming

Bungie helped define modern online shooters by creating exciting cooperative missions and competitive multiplayer modes. Destiny 2 continues to grow through regular updates, seasonal content, and expansions that keep players engaged.

---

## Famous Games

- Destiny
- Destiny 2
- Halo: Combat Evolved
- Halo 2
- Halo 3

---

## Why Bungie Matters

Bungie has earned a reputation for creating exciting shooter games that encourage teamwork and long-term progression. Its games have influenced many multiplayer titles that followed.

> "Great adventures are even better with a fireteam."
