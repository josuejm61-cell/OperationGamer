# Mojang Studios

## Overview

Mojang Studios is a Swedish video game developer best known for creating Minecraft. Founded in 2009, the company quickly became successful because of Minecraft's unique sandbox gameplay. In 2014, Mojang Studios became part of Microsoft while continuing to develop and update Minecraft.

---

## Contributions to Gaming

Minecraft changed the gaming industry by encouraging creativity, exploration, and building. The game allows players to create their own adventures, making it one of the best-selling video games of all time.

---

## Famous Games

- Minecraft
- Minecraft Dungeons
- Minecraft Legends

---

## Why Mojang Matters

Mojang Studios proved that a simple idea could become one of the biggest games in history. Minecraft continues to receive updates and remains popular with millions of players around the world.
