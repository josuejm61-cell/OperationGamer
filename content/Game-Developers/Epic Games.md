# Epic Games

## Overview

Epic Games is an American video game developer and publisher founded in 1991. The company is best known for creating Fortnite and developing Unreal Engine, one of the most widely used game engines in the video game industry. Epic Games has become one of the biggest names in gaming through its popular games and powerful development tools.

---

## Contributions to Gaming

Epic Games has helped shape modern gaming by creating Fortnite, which introduced live in-game events, seasonal content, and cross-platform play. Unreal Engine has also allowed thousands of developers to create games using advanced graphics and realistic physics.

---

## Famous Games

- Fortnite
- Rocket League (publisher)
- Unreal Tournament
- Gears of War (original developer)

---

## Why Epic Games Matters

Epic Games continues to influence the gaming industry through both its games and its technology. Fortnite remains one of the world's most popular online games, while Unreal Engine powers many successful games developed by other studios.
