# Rockstar Games

## Overview

Rockstar Games is an American video game publisher founded in 1998. The company is known for creating large open-world games that emphasize storytelling, exploration, and player freedom. Rockstar has produced several of the best-selling video games in history.

---

## Contributions to Gaming

Rockstar Games changed the gaming industry by creating detailed open worlds where players can explore, complete missions, and interact with realistic environments. Its games are known for their high-quality storytelling and attention to detail.

---

## Famous Games

- Grand Theft Auto V
- Red Dead Redemption 2
- Bully
- Max Payne 3

---

## Why Rockstar Games Matters

Rockstar Games has raised expectations for open-world games through its detailed environments and memorable stories. Its games continue to influence developers around the world.
