# First-Person Shooters

## Overview

First-person shooter (FPS) games place players directly into the action through the character's point of view. These games often focus on teamwork, fast reactions, and accurate aiming.

---

## Common Features

- First-person perspective
- Online multiplayer
- Team-based gameplay
- Competitive matches
- Weapon customization

---

## Popular Examples

- Destiny 2

---

## Why It Is Popular

FPS games provide exciting action and competitive gameplay. Many players enjoy improving their skills while working together with teammates.

> "Teamwork and accuracy lead to success."
