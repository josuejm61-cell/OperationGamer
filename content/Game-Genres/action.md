# Action

## Overview

Action games focus on fast gameplay that challenges a player's reflexes, timing, and coordination. Players often battle enemies, complete missions, or overcome obstacles while progressing through different levels. Action games are one of the most popular genres because they provide exciting gameplay and constant challenges.

---

## Common Features

- Fast-paced gameplay
- Combat
- Boss battles
- Missions and objectives
- Skill-based challenges

---

## Popular Examples

- Fortnite
- Super Mario Bros.
- Pac-Man

---

## Why It Is Popular

Action games keep players engaged through exciting gameplay and rewarding challenges. They are easy to start but often require practice to master.

> "Quick thinking and fast reactions lead to victory."
