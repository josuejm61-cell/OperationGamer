# Adventure

## Overview

Adventure games focus on exploration, storytelling, and solving puzzles. Players often travel through large worlds, complete quests, and discover hidden secrets while following an engaging story.

---

## Common Features

- Exploration
- Puzzle-solving
- Story-driven gameplay
- Character progression
- Hidden secrets

---

## Popular Examples

- Minecraft
- The Legend of Zelda
- Tears of the Kingdom

---

## Why It Is Popular

Adventure games reward players for exploring and discovering new locations. Many players enjoy the freedom and sense of accomplishment that comes from completing quests and solving puzzles.

> "Every adventure begins with a single step."
