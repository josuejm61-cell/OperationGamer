# Role-Playing Games

## Overview

Role-playing games (RPGs) allow players to control a character who grows stronger by completing quests, earning experience, and collecting better equipment. Many RPGs include large worlds, interesting stories, and character customization.

---

## Common Features

- Character progression
- Quests
- Equipment upgrades
- Exploration
- Storytelling

---

## Popular Examples

- Destiny 2
- Tears of the Kingdom

---

## Why It Is Popular

RPGs give players a sense of progression as they become stronger and unlock new abilities. Many players enjoy the long adventures and detailed stories these games provide.

> "Every quest makes your character stronger."
