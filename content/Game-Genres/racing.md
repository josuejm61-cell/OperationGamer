# Racing

## Overview

Racing games challenge players to compete against others by driving vehicles across different tracks. Success depends on speed, control, and learning each course.

---

## Common Features

- Competitive races
- Different vehicles
- Multiplayer modes
- Time trials
- Skill-based driving

---

## Popular Examples

- Mario Kart World

---

## Why It Is Popular

Racing games are fun because every race feels different. Players can improve their driving skills, compete with friends, and unlock new vehicles or characters.

> "The finish line is only the beginning of the next race."
