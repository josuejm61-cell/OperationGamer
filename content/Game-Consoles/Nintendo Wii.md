# Nintendo Wii

## Overview

The Nintendo Wii was released in 2006 and became one of Nintendo's best-selling consoles. It introduced motion controls that allowed players to interact with games by moving the controller instead of only pressing buttons.

---

## Features

- Motion controls
- Family-friendly games
- Multiplayer gaming
- Easy to learn
- Innovative gameplay

---

## Why It Is Popular

The Wii introduced many new people to video games because its motion controls were simple and fun. It became a favorite console for families and parties.

> "Gaming became more active with the Wii."
