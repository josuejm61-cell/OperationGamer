## What are the primary resources being organized?

The main resources organized on the Avatar Wiki are individual articles. Each article focuses on a specific topic within the Avatar universe, such as character, episode, nation, location, bending style, or organization. Many articles also include images, info boxes, references, and links to related topics, making it easy for readers to learn about every part of the Avatar universe. The page is split into very different categories that highlight differences, like the bending styles of different nations and what sets them apart. Apart from The Order of The White Lotus, there is the Order of The Red Lotus, which is the opposite: there should be order, and no one is above another. Other examples include live action, animation, comics, books, and games.

The Avatar Wiki website primarily supports research, browsing, and fact-checking. ## What classification system does it use?

The website primarily uses a structured classification system through categories and subcategories. The articles are grouped into larger topics such as Characters, Organizations, Episodes, and Locations. These larger sense topics are organized and categorized by the subject you are looking for, whether it is information or fan theory.  
Now, within those categories, users can freely narrow their search to more specific subjects. For example, the Organizations category contains many groups, such as the Kyoshi Warriors, the Red Lotus, and the Order of the White Lotus. I think this system works well because fans can either search for exactly what they need or browse categories to discover new information.

---

## How are relationships between resources represented?

Relationships between articles are represented in several ways. The most common method in all pages, not just this one, is the use of hyperlinks within text that connect readers to related articles. Like so, we have been learning to use the links to go back to the other pages. The pages also include categories at the bottom, info boxes linking to related topics, navigation templates, and "See also" sections when appropriate. These connections create a network of information that allows users to move naturally from one topic to another without needing to return to the main page.

Overall, I think the Avatar Wiki is organized for its intended purpose. The combination of articles and categories, along with well-organized hyperlinks, makes it easy to find information quickly while also encouraging us to explore related topics of interest. Whether you are looking for a single character or researching the history of the four nations, the website provides an organized and easy-to-use knowledge base that supports both quick fact-checking and more detailed research.
