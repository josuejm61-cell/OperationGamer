# Pac-Man

## Overview

Pac-Man is one of the most recognizable arcade games ever made. Developed by **Namco** and released in 1980, the game became a worldwide success and helped make video games popular with players of all ages. Even today, Pac-Man remains one of the most iconic characters in gaming history.

---

## Gameplay

Players control Pac-Man as he moves through a maze eating pellets while avoiding four colorful ghosts. Eating a Power Pellet allows Pac-Man to temporarily eat the ghosts for extra points. As players complete levels, the ghosts become faster and more challenging.

The game is easy to learn but becomes difficult as players try to survive longer and earn higher scores.

---

## Features

- Classic arcade gameplay
- Maze navigation
- High-score competition
- Simple controls
- Increasing difficulty

---

## Why It Is Important

Pac-Man helped introduce video games to a much larger audience during the early years of arcade gaming. Its simple gameplay and memorable characters made it one of the most successful arcade games ever created, and it continues to influence games today.

> "Sometimes the simplest games become the most legendary."
