# Tetris

## Overview

Tetris is one of the most famous puzzle games ever created. Designed by **Alexey Pajitnov** in 1984, the game challenges players to arrange falling blocks into complete horizontal lines. Its simple concept and addictive gameplay have made it one of the best-selling video games of all time.

---

## Gameplay

In Tetris, different shaped blocks, called tetrominoes, fall from the top of the screen. Players rotate and move the pieces to create complete rows. When a row is completed, it disappears and the player earns points. As the game continues, the blocks fall faster, making it more difficult to keep the board from filling up.

The game is easy to learn but takes practice to master, making it enjoyable for players of all skill levels.

---

## Features

- Classic puzzle gameplay
- Simple controls
- Increasing difficulty
- High-score competition
- Fast-paced decision making

---

## Why It Is Important

Tetris became one of the most successful puzzle games in history because of its simple but challenging gameplay. It has been released on dozens of gaming systems and mobile devices, introducing millions of people around the world to puzzle games.

> "A few simple blocks created one of the greatest games ever made."
