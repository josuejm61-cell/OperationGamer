# Super Mario Bros.

## Overview

Super Mario Bros. is one of the most influential video games ever created. Developed by **Nintendo** and released in 1985 for the Nintendo Entertainment System (NES), it introduced millions of players to Mario and helped shape the future of platform games.

---

## Gameplay

Players control Mario as he travels through the Mushroom Kingdom to rescue Princess Peach from Bowser. Along the way, players jump over obstacles, defeat enemies, collect coins, and discover hidden areas. The game becomes more challenging as players progress through each world.

---

## Features

- Side-scrolling platform gameplay
- Memorable levels
- Power-ups like the Super Mushroom and Fire Flower
- Hidden secrets
- Two-player mode

---

## Why It Is Important

Super Mario Bros. helped revive the video game industry after the Video Game Crash of 1983. It became one of the best-selling games of its time and introduced many gameplay ideas that are still used in platform games today.

> "Mario's first adventure inspired generations of gamers."
