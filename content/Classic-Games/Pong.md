# Pong

## Overview

Pong is one of the first successful video games ever created. Developed by **Atari** and released in 1972, it helped introduce arcade gaming to millions of people. Although the game is simple by today's standards, it played a major role in the growth of the video game industry.

---

## Gameplay

In Pong, two players control paddles on opposite sides of the screen and try to hit a ball back and forth. The goal is to make the other player miss the ball to score points. As the game continues, players must react quickly and time their movements carefully.

Its simple controls make Pong easy to learn, but matches can become very competitive.

---

## Features

- Two-player gameplay
- Simple controls
- Arcade competition
- Fast-paced matches
- Historic game design

---

## Why It Is Important

Pong helped prove that video games could become a successful form of entertainment. It inspired many future games and helped establish Atari as one of the first major video game companies. Today, it is remembered as one of the games that started the video game industry.

> "Every great gaming journey had to start somewhere."
