# Arcade Era

## Overview

The Arcade Era was one of the first major periods in video game history. During the late 1970s and early 1980s, people visited arcades to play games on large machines using coins or tokens. Arcades became popular places where friends gathered to compete for high scores and enjoy the newest games.

---

## Key Characteristics

- Coin-operated machines
- High-score competitions
- Popular public gathering places
- Simple but challenging gameplay
- Birth of many classic games

---

## Why It Is Important

The Arcade Era helped introduce millions of people to video games. Many legendary games, including Pac-Man and Pong, became popular during this time and inspired future generations of game developers.

> "Arcades helped launch the video game industry."
