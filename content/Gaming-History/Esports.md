# Esports

## Overview

Esports, or electronic sports, are organized competitive video game tournaments where professional players and teams compete for prizes and championships. Millions of people watch esports events online and in person each year.

---

## Common Features

- Professional teams
- Live tournaments
- Prize money
- Online streaming
- Competitive gameplay

---

## Why It Is Important

Esports has transformed gaming into a global competitive activity. It has created career opportunities for players, coaches, broadcasters, and event organizers while bringing gaming communities together.

> "Competition pushes players to improve and inspire others."
