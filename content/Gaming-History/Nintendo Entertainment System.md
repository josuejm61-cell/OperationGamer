# Nintendo Entertainment System

## Overview

The Nintendo Entertainment System (NES) was released by Nintendo in the 1980s and helped revive the video game industry after the Video Game Crash of 1983. It introduced players to many classic games that are still popular today.

---

## Features

- Home gaming console
- Classic Nintendo games
- Improved graphics for its time
- Reliable game quality
- Easy-to-use controllers

---

## Why It Is Important

The NES helped restore confidence in video games and introduced many franchises that continue today, including Super Mario Bros. and The Legend of Zelda.

> "The NES changed gaming forever."
