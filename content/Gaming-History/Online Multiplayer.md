# Online Multiplayer

## Overview

Online multiplayer allows players to connect over the internet and play games together from different locations. It has become one of the most important features in modern gaming because it allows friends and players around the world to compete or cooperate.

---

## Common Features

- Team-based gameplay
- Online matchmaking
- Voice chat
- Cooperative missions
- Competitive modes

---

## Why It Is Important

Online multiplayer has changed the way people play games by making it easier to connect with others. Many popular games today are built around online communities.

> "Gaming brings people together, no matter where they are."
