# The Video Game Crash of 1983

## Overview

The Video Game Crash of 1983 was one of the biggest challenges in gaming history. Too many low-quality games entered the market, causing consumer confidence to fall and video game sales to decline significantly.

---

## What Happened?

Many companies released games too quickly without focusing on quality. As a result, players stopped buying games, and several companies struggled or closed. The industry eventually recovered with the release of the Nintendo Entertainment System (NES).

---

## Why It Is Important

The crash taught game companies the importance of quality and helped shape the future of the video game industry.
